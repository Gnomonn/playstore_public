Pawn Rush — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-07

Pawn Rush ("we", "the app") respects your privacy. This policy explains what data the app handles and why. By using Pawn Rush you agree to this policy.

Who we are

Developer: Gnomonn. Contact: gnomonn.lab@gmail.com.

Data we collect

  • Advertising data — our ad provider (Google AdMob) may collect a device advertising ID (AAID), approximate location (derived from IP address), and app interaction / diagnostic data in order to serve and measure rewarded ads.
  • Online leaderboard & authentication data — we use Unity Gaming Services (UGS) to provide anonymous player authentication and public leaderboards. This includes an anonymous player identifier, the player-submitted display name (nickname), and gameplay scores/ranks.

Data we do NOT collect

  • Your real name, email address, or phone number — authentication is strictly anonymous.
  • Your contacts, photos, files, or precise GPS location.
  • Payment information — the game does not process direct payments.
  • Any sensitive personal information.

Advertising

Pawn Rush shows optional rewarded ads via Google AdMob (for example, to continue a run after game over). AdMob may use advertising and device identifiers to serve personalized or non-personalized ads and to measure performance. Where required (e.g. in the EEA/UK), we request consent before serving personalized ads. See Google's policy: policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Third-party services

  • Google AdMob — advertising. Privacy policy.
  • Unity Gaming Services (UGS) — anonymous authentication and leaderboards. Unity Privacy Policy.

Data retention & deletion

We do not operate custom backend servers. Advertising data is handled by Google under its own retention policies. Leaderboard entries and anonymous player IDs are managed through Unity Gaming Services. Local game data can be removed at any time by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app. If you wish to request deletion of your leaderboard display name or score, contact gnomonn.lab@gmail.com.

Children

Pawn Rush is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy periodically. Material changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date above.

Contact

Questions or data requests: gnomonn.lab@gmail.com.
